INTERASTRAL PEACE CORPORATION

A company created to support the Aeon Qlipoth. It has since become the largest economic entity in the cosmos.

The Interastral Peace Corporation, abbreviated as the IPC, is an intergalactic mega corporation responsible for the economy of the entire cosmos.
